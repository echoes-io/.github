# Timeline Master

## When to Use

Activate this skill when you need:
- What year/month an arc takes place
- How old Nic or GioGio is during a specific arc
- Whether two arcs overlap chronologically
- Season/weather context for a scene
- Tech availability (what phone existed in year X)

## Complete Reference

See [timeline-master.md](references/timeline-master.md) for the full chronological timeline.

## Quick Rules

- Verify ages in the master before writing — don't calculate yourself
- Seasons matter for outfit and atmosphere descriptions
- Tech must match the year (no iPhone 15 in a 2019 arc)
- If two arcs are simultaneous, Echi Paralleli rules apply
