# Echi Paralleli — Cross-Reference Architecture

## The Rule

**Each arc is autonomous.** A reader who reads ONLY the ALE arc must have a complete, satisfying experience. They must NEVER feel they're missing context from another arc.

## What's Forbidden

- ❌ Direct references to events in other arcs ("like that time in Sicily with Cri")
- ❌ Characters from other arcs appearing unexpectedly
- ❌ Plot points that require knowledge from another arc
- ❌ Emotional arcs that only make sense with cross-arc context
- ❌ Resolution of tension set up in a different arc

## What's Allowed (Oblique Echoes)

- ✅ A gesture Nic makes that a multi-arc reader recognizes but a single-arc reader doesn't miss
- ✅ A thought pattern ("I've been here before" — without specifying when/with whom)
- ✅ A physical reaction that hints at past experiences
- ✅ A word or phrase that carries different weight for multi-timeline readers
- ✅ Nic's scars (emotional patterns) that manifest differently with different women

## The Principle

The echo is **texture**, not **plot**. It rewards the multi-timeline reader without punishing the single-arc reader.

## Practical Examples

**FORBIDDEN:**
> "The last time I'd been in a hotel like this was with Cri in Cervinia. Different woman, same vertigo."

**ALLOWED:**
> "Hotels did something to me. Something about the anonymity of a room that wasn't mine."

The first requires CRI context. The second is a standalone observation that CRI readers recognize but ALE readers simply read as character depth.

## Cross-Timeline Notes

- Anima-Nic has NO Eros scars. Eros arcs don't exist in Anima's universe.
- Glow-Nic has patterns but doesn't feel them. He's lighter.
- Pulse can reference emotions more freely (it's confessional) but still never names other arcs directly.
